The lab received good news on two recent grant applications:

* A grant from the [Royalty Reseach Fund (RRF)](https://www.washington.edu/research/or/royalty-research-fund-rrf/) that will fund our studies of Merkel cell development. The RRF program is funded from royalty and licensing fee income generated by the University of Washington's technology transfer program. 
* An Innovation Pilot Award from the [Institute for Stem Cell and Regenerative Medecine (ISCRM)](https://iscrm.uw.edu/research/research-award-opportunities/) that will support our project on skin wound healing. The Innovation Pilot Award is supported by resources from the State of Washington.

Thank you to RRF and ISCRM for your support!
