Our manuscript from Ahlan Ferdous and colleagues investigating how Merkel cells regenerate is now posted on [bioRxiv](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.09.16.752174). We use singe-cell RNA-sequencing to track the molecular and cellular changes associated with how keratinocyte progenitors give rise to these specialized mechanosensory cells following skin injury in zebrafish. We also compare our data with mouse and human data to identify a core, conserved gene expression signature of Merkel cells. Check it out!
